# CareerCompass Career Guide

## Choosing a career path
Match roles to the overlap of what you enjoy, what you are good at, and what the market needs. Try short projects in two or three candidate fields before committing.

## Building a portfolio
Employers value evidence. Publish 2–4 well-documented projects on GitHub with a README explaining the problem, approach, results and how to run it. Quantify results where possible.

## Writing an ATS-friendly resume
Use a single-column layout, standard headings (Summary, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education), plain fonts, and keywords from the job description that truthfully describe your experience. Start bullets with action verbs and include numbers.

## Internships and first jobs
Apply broadly, tailor each resume, and follow up. Referrals significantly increase interview rates — reach out to alumni on LinkedIn with a short, specific message.

## Interview preparation
Practice the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioural questions. For technical roles, practice data structures, SQL queries, and explaining your projects end to end.

## Learning effectively
Learn by building. Follow a weekly schedule, combine one course with one project, and review progress every week. Consistency beats intensity.

## Career transitions
Map transferable skills, close the most important gaps first, and build one project in the new field that connects to your previous experience.

## Certifications
Certifications help most in cloud (AWS, Azure), security (Security+, Google Cybersecurity) and data (Google Data Analytics). Pair them with hands-on projects.
